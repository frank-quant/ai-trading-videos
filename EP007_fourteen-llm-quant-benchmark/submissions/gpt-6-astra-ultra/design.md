For each pair, the bullish factor at candle u is:

`(log(close[u]) - log(close[u-N])) / (std(log-return[u-83:u], ddof=1) * sqrt(N))`.

The selected N is 12 candles, or 48 hours. The rolling volatility window is fixed at 84 candles, or 14 days. Scaling by recent volatility reduces the tendency to choose assets solely because their price fluctuations are larger. The cross-section measures relative strength; it does not impose an absolute positive-return requirement. Nonpositive prices, zero volatility and zero-volume observations produce unavailable scores instead of fabricated observations.

The supplied `CrossSectionalBase` constructs cross-sectional percentile ranks and applies its original `shift(1)` to both scores and ranks. That protection is unchanged. On signal row t, all ranked information is from t-1 or earlier. Freqtrade subsequently executes signals on the next candle, retaining an additional conservative candle delay. A subclass cache signature includes each pair's data coverage so appended candles and truncated-prefix audits rebuild the panel. No full-sample normalization, forward shift, centered window or future-dependent ranking is used.

On a complete, untied cross-section, enter the top seven ranks long and the bottom seven short. Strict `rank > 0.65` selects seven longs; `rank <= 0.35` selects seven shorts. Ties or missing scores can change actual counts. Exit a long below rank 0.55 and a short above rank 0.45. Rank exits require 24 hours of holding. The fixed 20% stop and forced/safety exits bypass that minimum immediately. ROI and trailing exits are disabled so rank deterioration determines ordinary exits. Actual exposure is not constrained to dollar or beta neutrality: positions persist within the exit bands, stake sizes vary, and long/short trades finish at different times.

The 4h timeframe permits timely relative-strength updates while the minimum hold and rank bands suppress immediate switching. Nevertheless, the selected parameters are costly: the final average holding period is 1 day 20 hours 55 minutes, with 15,009 trades over the combined interval. Two-sided turnover is 419.95 starting-wallet multiples/year on TRAIN and 372.71 on VALIDATION. Turnover control did not make this a low-turnover strategy.
