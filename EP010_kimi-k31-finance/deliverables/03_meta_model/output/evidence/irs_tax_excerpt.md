# U.S. federal corporate tax-rate evidence

Source: Internal Revenue Service, Internal Revenue Bulletin 2018-18.
URL: https://www.irs.gov/irb/2018-18_IRB

Section 13001(a) of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act amended section 11(a) to impose a 21% corporate tax rate, effective for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2017. The model uses 21% only for the WACC debt tax shield; forecast effective tax rates remain separate company/model assumptions.
